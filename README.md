## What Microtensor is

Microtensor is a Bittensor subnet that produces specialist AI systems: small language models fine tuned for one real task, wrapped in the code that makes them reliable, and proven on hidden tests before anyone relies on them. The network rewards the people who build the best system for each task.

## Why we exist

Frontier models answer everything, at frontier prices and frontier latency. Most real work is narrow: compact an agent's context, read a document page, write one database query. A small model trained for that one job runs on a single GPU, a laptop or a phone at a fraction of the cost, once someone has proven it is good enough. Microtensor is where that proof happens, in the open, by a decentralised network rather than on a vendor's word.

## What we do

- Define tasks worth a specialist, with hidden tests nobody can train on.
- Reward miners for building and improving the systems that solve them.
- Verify every result through validators, so a score is earned, never claimed.
- Make the proven systems available to anyone who needs them.

## Our goal

Every narrow task that runs on a frontier model today gets a specialist that is smaller, faster and cheaper, with its proof public.
